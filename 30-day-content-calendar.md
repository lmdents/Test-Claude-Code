# 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar
**Brand:** LM Dents — Social Media Strategy
**Platforms:** Instagram (reach) + LinkedIn (authority)
**Phase:** Foundation Building
**Voice:** Professional, direct, educational, authentic

---

## How to Read This Calendar

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| IG | Instagram |
| LI | LinkedIn |
| Reach | New audience discovery |
| Trust | Build credibility & authority |
| Convert | Drive inquiries or sales |

**Format options:**
- **Reel** — 15–60s video, fast hook, high reach potential
- **Carousel** — Swipeable slides, strong for saves and shares
- **Single Image** — Clean visual + strong caption
- **Long-form Post** — LinkedIn text, 150–400 words, structured
- **Short Post** — LinkedIn quick take, 3–8 lines
- **Story** — Temporary, conversational, polls or questions

## Week 1 — Plant the Flag (Days 1–7)
*Establish who you are, what you do, and who it's for.*

---

### Day 1
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** Introduction — who you are, what you do, who you help
**Format:** IG: Single Image | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Clear positioning. Make it instantly obvious who this is for.
**Goal:** Trust

Write it like this: open with a bold statement about the problem you solve. Briefly introduce yourself. State clearly who you help and what changes for them. Close with: *"Follow if you want to build a social media presence that actually works for your business."*

---

### Day 2
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "Why most local businesses are invisible online"
**Format:** Reel (talking head or text-on-screen)
**Angle:** Problem-awareness. Make your target audience feel seen.
**Goal:** Reach

Hook options:
- *"Why your dental practice has 0 new clients from Instagram"*
- *"Local businesses are losing clients to competitors who just post better content"*

Name 3–4 specific reasons: no strategy, no consistency, no clear message, wrong content type. End with a tease of the fix.

---

### Day 3
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "What most businesses get wrong about social media from Day 1"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Education + credibility. Position yourself as someone who thinks differently.
**Goal:** Trust

Open with a counterintuitive statement. Explain the difference between activity and strategy. Give 2–3 concrete examples of what strategic posting looks like vs. random posting. Close with a question: *"What's your biggest social media challenge right now?"*

---

### Day 4
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "The #1 mistake I see local businesses make on Instagram"
**Format:** Carousel (5–7 slides)
**Angle:** Education + practical. Something immediately actionable.
**Goal:** Trust

Slides:
1. Hook: *"The mistake killing your reach (and how to fix it)"*
2. The mistake: posting only about services and products
3. Why it doesn't work — people care about their problems, not your business
4. What to post instead: your client's pain points and goals
5. Bad caption vs. good caption side-by-side
6. CTA: *"Save this before you post again"*

---

### Day 5
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** "Why dental practices have a massive social media opportunity right now"
**Format:** IG: Reel | LI: Short Post
**Angle:** Niche-specific insight. Speaks directly to your ideal client.
**Goal:** Reach

Key points:
- 90% of dental practices post the same generic content
- Trust is the #1 buying factor in healthcare — social media builds that trust
- The few practices with real personality online are pulling ahead
- This gap won't last — the time to move is now

---

### Day 6
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "The 3 types of content every local business needs"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Framework-driven education. Give followers a mental model they can use.
**Goal:** Trust

The 3 types:
1. **Reach content** — hooks new people (tips, observations, relatable moments)
2. **Trust content** — makes people believe you know what you're doing (breakdowns, insights, analysis)
3. **Conversion content** — makes people take action (offers, CTAs, proof)

Close with: *"Most businesses post only one. The ones growing post all three."*

---

### Day 7
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** Build in public — why you're starting this and what you're doing
**Format:** IG: Single Image with personal caption | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Personal and authentic. Show the real journey, not the highlight reel.
**Goal:** Trust

Share: why you're building this brand, what you're learning in the first week, what excites and challenges you, your goal for the next 30 days. Transparency here builds faster trust than any polished post.

## Week 2 — Educate & Demonstrate Expertise (Days 8–14)
*Deliver consistent value. Become the go-to account for social media strategy in your niche.*

---

### Day 8
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "How to write a caption that gets people to stop scrolling"
**Format:** Carousel (6–8 slides)
**Angle:** Tactical education. Immediately usable.
**Goal:** Reach

Slides:
1. Hook: *"Most captions lose people in the first line. Here's why."*
2. The scroll test: you have 1–2 seconds
3. The 3-part formula: Hook → Value → CTA
4. Hook examples: bold claim, question, "I didn't know this until..."
5. What to avoid: never open with "We are excited to..." or "Today we..."
6. Before/after caption example
7. CTA: *"Try rewriting your next caption with this formula"*

---

### Day 9
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "You don't need to post every day to grow on social media"
**Format:** Short Post (myth-busting)
**Angle:** Contrarian take. Challenges a common belief.
**Goal:** Reach

Structure: open bold — *"Posting every day is not the strategy."* Explain why quality beats frequency for small accounts. Name the metrics that actually matter: saves, shares, profile visits. Close with your recommendation: 3–4 posts per week, done with intention.

---

### Day 10
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "I analyzed 10 dental practice Instagram profiles. Here's what I found."
**Format:** Reel (breakdown style)
**Angle:** Observation + analysis. Builds authority through demonstration.
**Goal:** Trust

Cover: the common patterns across most profiles (stock photos, generic posts, no personality), what the 1–2 good ones do differently, the single biggest missed opportunity, and one quick win anyone can implement today.

---

### Day 11
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** "The first thing to fix on your social media profile (before anything else)"
**Format:** IG: Single Image | LI: Short Post
**Angle:** Quick win. Easy, actionable, low barrier to implement.
**Goal:** Reach

Key points: most bios say what you are, not what you do for the client. Formula: [Who you help] + [What result you create] + [How to take the next step]. Show a bad example vs. a good one. Close: *"Fix this today. It takes 5 minutes."*

---

### Day 12
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "What studying 50+ local business accounts taught me about social media"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Research-backed insight. Shows you do the work.
**Goal:** Trust

Key findings:
- Businesses with personality outperform polished but generic accounts
- Consistency matters more than production quality
- The accounts with the most engagement ask questions and tell stories
- Local businesses that reference their community perform better
- Strategy always beats spontaneity over time

---

### Day 13
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "What a content calendar actually looks like — and why you need one"
**Format:** Carousel
**Angle:** Demystify the process. Make strategy feel accessible.
**Goal:** Trust

Slides:
1. *"Stop posting randomly. Start here."*
2. What a content calendar is — a plan, not a prison
3. The 3 pillars: theme, format, goal
4. A simple 1-week example
5. The difference it makes: less stress, more consistency, better results
6. CTA: *"Want a template? Comment 'CALENDAR' below."*

---

### Day 14
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** Build in public — Week 2 reflection
**Format:** IG: Story + Post | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Personal + transparent. Share what's working and what isn't.
**Goal:** Trust

Cover: which content performed best and why, what surprised you about the first two weeks, what you're changing going into Week 3, and one genuine insight or doubt you've been sitting with. Real reflections outperform polished summaries.

## Week 3 — Deepen Trust & Build Relatability (Days 15–21)
*Mix strategic education with personal content. Make people feel like they know you.*

---

### Day 15
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "What 'positioning' actually means — and why most businesses ignore it"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Concept breakdown. Deeper education for a professional audience.
**Goal:** Trust

Key points: positioning = owning a specific place in your client's mind. Most businesses try to appeal to everyone and stand out to no one. How to find yours: who, what result, why specifically you. Close with a simple exercise: *"Ask yourself — what would my best client say about me to a friend?"*

---

### Day 16
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "The social media opportunity local businesses are sleeping on"
**Format:** Reel (fast-paced)
**Angle:** Urgency + opportunity. Motivate action.
**Goal:** Reach

Talking points: most local service businesses have weak or no social presence. Clients ARE looking online before they book. The first business that shows up with real, trustworthy content wins by default. You don't need to be perfect — you need to be present and consistent.

---

### Day 17
**Platform:** LI + IG
**Idea:** "A local business that transformed its reputation with better content" (composite example)
**Format:** LI: Story-format Long Post | IG: Carousel
**Angle:** Narrative. Shows transformation without needing a live case study yet.
**Goal:** Trust

Structure:
- Before: small dental practice, inconsistent posts, no strategy, no engagement
- The shift: defined 3 content pillars, consistent format, clear CTA
- The result: more profile visits, more DMs, more bookings
- The lesson: strategy + consistency = results. No ad spend required.

*(Frame clearly as a composite/illustrative example until you have real case studies.)*

---

### Day 18
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "3 questions every post should answer before you hit publish"
**Format:** Carousel
**Angle:** Checklist/framework. Easy to save and apply immediately.
**Goal:** Trust + Reach

The 3 questions:
1. Who is this for? (If it's for everyone, it's for no one.)
2. What do I want them to feel, think, or do?
3. Why would they care about this right now?

Close with: *"Run your last 5 posts through this. What do you notice?"*

---

### Day 19
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "Unpopular opinion: most social media advice is making your business worse"
**Format:** Short Post / Opinion piece
**Angle:** Contrarian and intelligent. Sparks discussion.
**Goal:** Reach

Argument: generic advice (post more, use hashtags, go viral) ignores strategy entirely. The real question isn't *"how do I get more followers?"* — it's *"how do I attract the right clients?"* Strategy first. Tactics second.

---

### Day 20
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** "How I build a social media strategy from scratch — my actual process"
**Format:** IG: Carousel | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Process transparency. Show the work, build credibility.
**Goal:** Trust + soft Convert

The process:
1. Define the goal — what does success look like for this business?
2. Define the audience — who exactly are we talking to?
3. Build content pillars — 3–4 themes that serve the goal
4. Choose formats that match the platform
5. Create a 30-day posting plan
6. Measure and adjust after 4 weeks

Showing your process makes people want to hire you to do it for them.

---

### Day 21
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** Build in public — 3-week check-in
**Format:** IG: Single Image or Reel | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Raw and honest. Share what's working, what isn't, and what you're learning.
**Goal:** Trust

Cover: key metrics from Week 1–3 (reach, engagement, followers), your biggest insight so far, what you're doing differently in Week 4, and a question for your audience: *"What content has been most useful to you so far?"*

## Week 4 — Soft Conversion & Direct Invitation (Days 22–30)
*Turn trust into action. Introduce your offer naturally. Make the next step obvious.*

---

### Day 22
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "The businesses I work with have one thing in common"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Ideal client profile. Speak directly to your target audience.
**Goal:** Convert

What to write: describe the common situation — established business, knows social media matters, hasn't found a system that works. What holds them back: time, strategy, not knowing what to post. What changes when they have a clear plan. Soft CTA: *"If this sounds like you, let's talk."*

---

### Day 23
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** "What working with me actually looks like"
**Format:** IG: Carousel | LI: Long-form Post
**Angle:** Transparency about your offer. No overselling.
**Goal:** Convert

Cover:
- The problem you solve (no strategy, no consistency, unclear message)
- The process (discovery → strategy → content plan → execution support)
- What you don't do (you're a strategist, not a content mill)
- Who it's for (local business owners ready to invest in their visibility)
- How to start (DM, link in bio, 30-min consultation call)

---

### Day 24
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "Organic vs. paid social — which one should you start with?"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Decision-framework education. Helps your audience think clearly.
**Goal:** Trust

Key argument: paid works faster but stops the moment you stop paying. Organic is slower but compounds over time. If you don't have a working message, paying to amplify it wastes money. Best sequence: build organic foundation → validate what works → amplify with paid.

---

### Day 25
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "The most common question I get about social media strategy"
**Format:** Reel (FAQ-style, direct to camera)
**Angle:** Meet your audience where they are. Answer what they're already asking.
**Goal:** Reach + Trust

Suggested question: *"How long does it take to see results from social media?"*

Answer framework:
- Honest timeline: 3–6 months for meaningful organic growth
- What affects the speed: consistency, content quality, niche size, platform
- What results should actually look like at each stage
- The biggest mistake: stopping too early

---

### Day 26
**Platform:** IG + LI
**Idea:** Free resource — "The 5-point social media audit you can do in 10 minutes"
**Format:** IG: Carousel | LI: Long-form Post with checklist
**Angle:** High-value free resource. Creates goodwill and drives saves and shares.
**Goal:** Reach + Convert

The 5-point audit:
1. Profile — does your bio communicate value in 3 seconds?
2. Consistency — are you posting regularly and with variety?
3. Message clarity — would a stranger understand what you do instantly?
4. Engagement rate — are people interacting or just scrolling past?
5. CTA presence — are you ever asking people to take the next step?

Close with: *"Run this audit. If you score below 3/5 — let's talk."*

---

### Day 27
**Platform:** LI
**Idea:** "My philosophy on social media for service businesses"
**Format:** Long-form Post
**Angle:** Point of view / manifesto. Show your values and thinking.
**Goal:** Trust

What to write: why you believe strategy matters more than virality for service businesses. What "good" social media actually means: consistent, honest, client-focused. What you're against: vanity metrics, generic content, empty advice. Your vision: every local business that deserves clients should have the tools to reach them.

### Day 28
**Platform:** IG
**Idea:** "Here's exactly how I would fix this dental practice's Instagram in 30 days"
**Format:** Carousel (strategy breakdown)
**Angle:** Hypothetical case study. Your expertise made visible.
**Goal:** Trust + Convert

Structure:
1. *"Let me show you what a 30-day turnaround looks like for a dental practice"*

3. Week 1: optimize profile + define 3 content pillars
4. Week 2: build trust content (team intro, process, FAQ)

### Day 29

**Idea:** Engagement post — ask your audience a direct question
**Format:** IG: Story poll + Feed Post | LI: Conversation-starter Post
**Angle:** Invert the dynamic. Let them talk.
**Goal:** Reach + Trust

Question options:

Reply to every single comment. This is relationship-building time — not broadcasting time.

Book a free 30-minute strategy call. Link in bio."* Close with genuine gratitude for the people who followed from the beginning.

## Execution Rules

1. **Write in your own voice** — use these as frameworks, not scripts
2. **Repurpose strategically** — IG carousels become LinkedIn text posts; Reels become short LinkedIn clips
3. **Engage on every post** — reply to every comment within the first hour, especially in the foundation phase
4. **Track what resonates** — after Week 1, note which posts got saved, shared, or commented on. Double down on those
5. **Don't skip the build-in-public posts** — they're often the highest-trust content you'll create at this stage
6. **Quality over quantity** — if a post isn't ready, delay it. A weak post does more damage than no post
