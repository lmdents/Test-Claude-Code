## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

LM Dents is not a typical marketing agency. It is a **specialist system provider** for dental practices — founded by someone who stands in both worlds: the dental clinic and the content strategy room. That dual identity is the brand's most powerful asset and must be the center of every piece of content, every pitch, every conversation.

The social media strategy for LM Dents is fundamentally **B2B content marketing**: the target customer is a dental practice owner or lead dentist, not a patient. This changes everything about platform choice, content angles, tone, and conversion mechanics.

**Core strategic premise:** Dental practice owners are not searching for "social media agency." They are scrolling Instagram and thinking "I know I need to post more, but I don't know what to say and I don't have time." LM Dents must meet them exactly there — with content that makes them feel understood, shows them what's possible, and makes Pius Lehmann the obvious expert they want guiding them.

## PART 2: TARGET AUDIENCE

### Primary Audience: The Dental Practice Owner / Lead Dentist

**Demographics:**
- Age: 32–58
- Gender: Mix (slight male skew in practice ownership, shifting)
- Location: Germany, Austria, Switzerland (DACH)
- Role: Praxis owner, managing dentist, practice partner

**Psychographics:** See Part 9 (Target Audience Deep-Dive) for the full breakdown of frustrations, desires, fears, and habits.

### Audience Segments

#### Segment A: "The Aware but Overwhelmed" (Largest segment — 45%)
- Knows social media matters for the practice
- Has started and stopped multiple times
- Either posts inconsistently or not at all
- Does not have time or staff capacity to do it properly
- **Key insight:** They don't need convincing. They need a system and someone to take ownership.

#### Segment B: "The Skeptic" (25%)
- Has tried an agency before — got generic posts, no results, wasted money
- Believes social media "doesn't work for dentists"
- Still quietly watches competitors getting traction
- **Key insight:** They need to see proof. Case studies, specific results, transparent methodology.

#### Segment C: "The Ambitious Grower" (20%)
- Younger practice or recently expanded
- Actively wants to differentiate and attract private patients (Privatpatienten)
- Willing to invest if they see a clear system and ROI path
- **Key insight:** Show them the vision. Premium patient acquisition. Practice as a brand.

#### Segment D: "The Delegator" (10%)
- Very busy — doesn't want to be involved in the content process
- Will pay well for full-service that requires minimal input
- **Key insight:** Emphasize the "Wir übernehmen alles" message. Reduce their cognitive load.

## PART 3: PLATFORM STRATEGY

### Platform Priority Stack

#### 1. LinkedIn (Primary — B2B Client Acquisition)
**Why this is #1:** Dental practice owners are professionals. They consume business and healthcare content on LinkedIn. Decision-makers justify B2B purchases after seeing thought leadership here.

- **Content role:** Establish Pius Lehmann as the definitive expert in dental social media strategy in the DACH market
- **Format mix:** 50% personal insight posts (text-heavy), 25% case study carousels, 15% short videos, 10% reposts with commentary
- **Posting frequency:** 4–5x per week
- **Voice:** Expert peer, not salesperson. Write as if sharing professional observations with colleagues.
- **Connection strategy:** Connect with dentists, practice managers, dental associations (BZÄK, KZBV members), dental school contacts, Dentalhandel

**LinkedIn is where trust is built and deals are started.**

#### 2. Instagram (Secondary — Visibility & Social Proof)
**Why:** Dentists scroll Instagram too. It is also where practices can see exactly what LM Dents would produce for them — Instagram functions as a live portfolio.

- **Content role:** Portfolio showcase + humanize the brand + attract inbound from practices who find LM Dents while researching their own Instagram strategy
- **Format mix:** 40% educational carousels (for dental practices), 30% Reels (process, results, insights), 20% Stories (daily touchpoints), 10% static posts
- **Posting frequency:** 4–5x per week
- **Key tactic:** Post content *as if* you were managing a dental practice — this directly demonstrates capability. Every post is a pitch deck.

#### 3. TikTok (Tertiary — Organic Reach & Personal Brand)
**Why:** High organic reach. Dental content performs extremely well. Establishes Pius as a recognizable face in the dental community — dentists who are patients of TikTok will find him.

- **Content role:** Personal brand + fast educational content + organic reach engine
- **Format mix:** 100% short-form video
- **Posting frequency:** 3–4x per week
- **Key tactic:** Speak directly to dentists. "Wenn du Zahnarzt bist und das siehst..." hooks perform well in professional niches.

#### 4. YouTube (Long-Term Authority Asset — Start Month 4+)
**Why:** Evergreen search traffic. Dentists researching "Wie Social Media für Zahnarztpraxis" will find long-form content years after posting. Also a premium signal of expertise.

- **Content role:** Deep-dive tutorials, case study walkthroughs, "how we built a practice's social media" series
- **Posting frequency:** 1–2x per month
- **Key tactic:** YouTube is where you turn viewers into convinced buyers.

## PART 4: CONTENT DIRECTION

### The Master Content Framework

Every piece of content LM Dents produces serves one of four purposes:

```
EDUCATE → VALIDATE → DEMONSTRATE → CONVERT
```

- **Educate:** Teach dental practices what they don't know about social media
- **Validate:** Make them feel understood. Reflect their frustrations back at them accurately.
- **Demonstrate:** Show what excellent dental social media looks like (by doing it yourself)
- **Convert:** Make the next step (booking a discovery call) feel natural and low-risk

---

### Content Categories & Examples

#### Category 1: SYSTEM EDUCATION (30%)
*Teach the frameworks. Show how LM Dents thinks.*

**Topics:**
- "Warum die meisten Zahnarztpraxen auf Instagram scheitern" (Why most dental practices fail on Instagram)
- "Das 3-Säulen-Content-System für Zahnarztpraxen" (The 3-pillar content system)

> Falsche Frage.
> Die richtige Frage ist: "Welche Angst oder Frage hat unser Patient heute — und wie können wir sie heute beantworten?"
> Wenn du Social Media so denkst, löst sich das Content-Problem von selbst.
> Hier sind die 5 häufigsten Patientenfragen, die jede Praxis auf Instagram beantworten sollte: [→ Carousel]

#### Category 2: CASE STUDIES & RESULTS (25%)
*Social proof is the single most powerful conversion tool for a B2B service.*

**Format:** Before/after of a practice's social media presence. Metrics. What changed. How.

**Topics:**
- "Wie Praxis [X] in 6 Monaten von 0 auf 2.000 Instagram-Follower kam — und was das für neue Patienten bedeutete"

- "Vorher/Nachher: So sah der Instagram-Account einer Praxis aus, bevor und nachdem wir ein System eingebaut haben"

**Key rule:** Every case study must include a measurable outcome. Followers and engagement are vanity metrics. New patient inquiries, booking increases, and review count growth are business outcomes.

#### Category 3: FOUNDER AUTHORITY CONTENT (25%)
*People buy from people. Pius Lehmann's dual identity as a dental student AND strategist is the brand's unfair advantage. Use it constantly.*

**Topics:**

**Sample TikTok/Reel hook:**

#### Category 4: INDUSTRY INSIGHTS & TRENDS (10%)

**Topics:**

**Topics:**

## PART 5: GROWTH STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–3)
**Goal:** Build a consistent, professional presence. Establish the positioning. Reach first 500 LinkedIn connections + 300 Instagram followers in the dental niche.

**Actions:**
- Optimize all profiles: LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok
- Write and post 3 "pillar" long-form LinkedIn articles (these rank in search and get reshared)
- Film 5 core Reels establishing expertise (the "why I do this" story, the framework overview, a myth-buster, a case study preview, a "what bad dental social media looks like")
- Connect with 20–30 dental professionals on LinkedIn per week (personalized connection notes only)
- Reach out to 5 dental practices for a free initial analysis — use results as case study content
- Collect 3 written testimonials from any early clients or collaborators

**KPIs:** LinkedIn connections in dental sector, post impressions, profile views, website visits from social

### Phase 2: Credibility Building (Months 4–6)
**Goal:** First 3 paying clients. 1,000+ LinkedIn followers. First media mention or industry feature.

**Actions:**
- Publish first proper case study (with client permission and metrics)
- Apply to speak at or write for German dental publications (zm online, DZW, Dental Tribune)
- Launch a free resource: "Der Social Media Starter-Guide für Zahnarztpraxen" (PDF lead magnet)
- Run a LinkedIn event or live session: "Q&A: Social Media für Zahnarztpraxen — eure Fragen, meine Antworten"
- Introduce referral partnerships with dental suppliers, dental lab contacts, dental coaches

**KPIs:** Leads generated, discovery calls booked, conversion rate from call to client, cost per lead

### Phase 3: Scale & Authority (Months 7–12)
**Goal:** 5–10 active retainer clients. Recognized as the go-to dental social media expert in DACH. 3,000+ LinkedIn followers.

**Actions:**
- Launch waitlist or limited capacity messaging ("Wir nehmen nur 3 neue Praxen pro Quartal auf")
- Pitch for keynote at DGZMK, IDS (Internationale Dental-Schau), or regional dental association events
- Build an affiliate/referral network with dental coaches and practice consultants
- Consider hiring a content assistant or virtual assistant to scale output
- Explore launching a group program or online course: "Social Media für Zahnarztpraxen: Das komplette System"

## PART 7: BRAND VOICE & COMMUNICATION STYLE

### The Pius Lehmann Brand Voice
Pius is the face of LM Dents. The brand voice IS his voice. It should feel like reading messages from a highly competent, honest, and straight-talking colleague who happens to understand both dentistry and digital strategy at an unusual level.

| Quality | How It Sounds |
|---------|--------------|
| **Direct** | Says what he means. No corporate fluff. Gets to the point fast. |
| **Expert without ego** | Teaches freely. Doesn't protect knowledge. Gives real value in every post. |
| **Empathetic to dental realities** | Acknowledges the time pressure, patient load, staff challenges of running a practice. Never minimizes them. |
| **Ambitious but grounded** | Paints a vision of what excellent dental communication looks like — and maps out how to get there practically. |
| **German precision** | Clear structure. No vague promises. Specific frameworks. Measurable outcomes. |

### Language Decisions
- **Use du-Form** consistently — dental practice owners in this age range respond better to peer-level communication than formal Sie in a social context
- Write in German primarily — occasional English for global industry terms is acceptable
- Avoid Denglish (German + English mashups) beyond established industry terms (Content, Social Media, Engagement)
- Use numbered frameworks, clear headers, and lists — German business audience responds to structure

### Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Clean white + navy or deep teal (professional + trustworthy) + a warm accent (amber or coral — approachable)
- **Photography:** Real photos of Pius, real dental environments, no stock photos
- **Graphics:** Clean, minimal infographic style. Data and frameworks visualized clearly.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif — Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, or similar

### The Only Metric That Truly Matters
**Discovery calls booked.** Everything else is a leading indicator. Track the conversion path:
```
Impression → Profile Visit → DM or Link Click → Discovery Call → Client
```

Optimize backwards from the discovery call. If calls aren't converting, fix the pitch. If calls aren't being booked, fix the CTA. If profiles aren't being visited, fix the content.
